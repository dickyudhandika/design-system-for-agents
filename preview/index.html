01

Principles

Restraint over decoration. Every surface, border, and color carries a single, clear job.

  1. 01

    Warm Dark Ground

    The canvas uses #121110 with warm #1c1a18 and #2a2520 surfaces, never a cool or pure #000000 black. This keeps the editorial, tactile aesthetic on a dark ground.

  2. 02

    Ceiling at Medium (500)

    Typography avoids heavy weights (600/700/800 bold). Visual prominence is achieved through scale and contrast, maintaining elegance.

  3. 03

    Strict Radius Ceiling (4px–8px)

    No rounded-full pills or exaggerated bubbly radii. Clean, architectural corners capped to a maximum of 8px.

  4. 04

    Singular Primary Intent

    Indigo (#818cf8) is reserved for the primary action on a screen (once per view). Purple (#a78bfa) exclusively denotes claimed status.

04

Spacing

A tight, small-step scale for controls and cards, opening up at 24 and 32 for layout rhythm.

05

Radius

Corners stop at 8px. Pills and bubbly shapes are out; square edges handle structural lines.

Card Radius

8px

Applied to visual preview cards, dialog containers, empty states, and panels.

Button & Control Radius

8px

Applied to buttons, form inputs, select controls, and interactive chips. No pill shapes.

0px
